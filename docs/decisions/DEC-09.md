# DEC-09: New IP: a broke jelly takes hits through a gig app in a sun-fried satirical coastal world; absurd, PG-13, bad jellies caught in the act

## Context

The candy-town world (Gumdrop Hollow, the Gumdrop Jubilee, the Sugarplum Syndicate) came from the 2D game. The person rejected it as stale ('reinventing Candyland') and asked for a fresh IP: jellies, with the vibe of Rabbids, Team Buddies, Thronglets and a more PG-13 Minions, a funny reason to be taking people out, legally distinct from the Hitman sniper games, and not 'shoot baby jellies' but 'shoot bad-guy jellies, and look how funny it is seeing cute sweet characters be evil'. They then pointed at a small side-quest slice of GTA 6 as the base, and at Squanch Games for the humour: 'embrace insanity and absurdity'. They approved the result: 'lock that shit in, that's gold'.

## Decision

The setting is a sun-fried satirical coastal region of jellies (modern-Florida satire in spirit). The protagonist is a broke jelly who takes hits through a gig app to make rent; the app is the handler, jobs are gig cards, and the score is a star rating. Clients are wronged regular jellies. Targets are recognisable modern jerks (crypto bros, scam influencers, HOA presidents, resort owners) with visible bad deeds you can catch them in, and popping them in the act is rewarded. Innocents are sweet and costly to hit. The arc ends at the app's headquarters, whose founder is the final villain. Tone: Squanch-style absurd escalation plus Rabbids, Minions, Team Buddies and Thronglets; PG-13 means crude is fine but no sexual content, slurs, real people, brands or politics. The death style (squash, splat, the eyes survive and blink on the puddle) and witnesses-as-snitches carry over. docs/game-bible.md is the source of truth.

## Why

The person found the candy world old and on the nose, and wanted something fresh and theirs. Satire of modern life gives endless recognisable villains, so the player is never the bully, and the cute-evil contrast is the core joke. The gig app makes the protagonist's motive funny, modern and nothing like an agency assassin.

## Alternatives rejected

The candy-town Jubilee with the Sugarplum Syndicate: rejected as stale. A grannies' knitting circle as the agency, a sour outcast jelly doing pest removal, a candy-factory quality inspector recalling 'sour' batches: pitched, not chosen once the GTA 6 slice idea came up.

## Consequences

Gumdrop Hollow, the Jubilee and the Syndicate are retired; the 2D game keeps them until it retires, and its rules and systems are still mined for the sim port, but not its names. The world palette moves from candy pastels to a sun-bleached coast while keeping the clay-diorama style; the person approves it on the boardwalk concept. Names (town, app, protagonist, title) are working until the person picks, and need a trademark check before launch. Legal lines are written in the bible for both Hitman and GTA.
