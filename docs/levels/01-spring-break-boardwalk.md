# Level 1: Spring Break boardwalk

Status: **design brief, locked direction** (DEC-09, DEC-10). Names are working names; the person can rename anyone. This level is the vertical slice: everything here is built to final quality, and its archetypes become the library for levels 2 to 20.

## The job

**Gig card:** *Removal × 3. Palmetto Point boardwalk, Spring Break weekend. Surge pricing ×2.4. Customer note: "These three ruined my retirement. make it look like an accident pls 🙏"*

**The client:** Doris Pembrook, a retiree who put her condo savings into a crypto coin, $JIGGLE, that went to zero by lunchtime. She's on the beach all day, so popping her is possible, and the app will have notes about it.

**The targets:**

| Target | Who they are | Look (reads at full zoom-out) | Bad deeds you can catch them in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Brayden "Moonboy" Kessler** | Crypto bro launching $JIGGLE on the pier. | Lemon yellow. Backwards cap, gold chain, aviators, phone always out. | Sells $JIGGLE to retirees. Pours his energy drink on the kids' sandcastle. Laughs at Doris when the coin crashes. |
| **Skylar Lux** | Influencer running fake giveaways. | Pink. Floppy sun hat, cat-eye sunglasses, selfie stick. | Shoves a kid out of her shot. Fakes drowning for content (the lifeguards all run). Steals Kazoo Kevin's tip jar "for a prank". |
| **"Big" Rick Gillis** | Owner of the Grand Flamingo Resort. Rigs the volleyball final, keeps a gator in the resort pool, is evicting the bait shop. | Big, orange. Captain's hat, cigar (3D), Hawaiian shirt paint. | Collects "fees" from the vendors. Bribes the volleyball ref. Tears up the bait shop's lease in front of Lorna. |

All three end the day on Rick's yacht, the *Moonshot*, which leaves at the fireworks. If it sails with a target aboard, that target escapes.

## The space

You're on the roof of the Sea Breeze Motor Inn, across the street, looking out over the boardwalk, the beach and the sea. Five zones, left to right roughly as the scope sees them:

1. **The resort pool deck** (west). The Grand Flamingo Resort: a big pool with Mr. Chompers the gator in a "decorative" lagoon beside it, a tiki bar, a DJ booth, foam cannons, a gate with bouncers.
2. **The boardwalk strip** (across the middle, nearest the perch). Margarita stand, hot dog cart, airbrush T-shirt shack, arcade with a claw machine, Gator Gus's mini-golf, a souvenir shop, a living statue, and the "WELCOME SPRING BREAKERS" banner hanging over the boardwalk exit by two chains.
3. **The beach** (the centre, the biggest zone). The volleyball court, two lifeguard towers, sunbathers under umbrellas, the sandcastle, coconut palms, the ice cream cart.
4. **The pier** (east). $JIGGLE's launch booth, a giant inflatable crab ("Shelly") tethered above it, the bait shop, fishing spots, a bench where the one-man band plays.
5. **The water and marina** (the far edge). Jet-ski rental dock, the parasail boat, the *Moonshot* yacht, the fireworks barge, and one lost kayaker.

**Sightlines:** the perch sees most of the beach and boardwalk openly. The pool deck is partly behind the resort's palms. The yacht is far, so shots there need patience and zoom. The pier booth sits under the inflatable crab.

## The guards: lifeguards

This level's guard type is the **lifeguard**, plus a few others.

- **Three lifeguards** (two towers and one at the pool). Each sweeps a vision cone with binoculars. **Anything that happens in the water is seen**, almost at once, inside their cone: the opposite of the 2D game's ponds, which hid pops. The water is watched; the foam isn't.
- **The whistle.** A lifeguard who sees something blows the whistle, and every crowd jelly nearby turns to look where they're looking. Then they run to the spot.
- **Their weakness:** they always respond to someone drowning. Skylar's fake drowning at 5:45 pulls all of them off their towers. That's a window.
- **The beach patrol** (one officer on an ATV) loops the sand and investigates puddles and screams.
- **The resort bouncers** (two) guard the pool gate. They watch the deck but can't see through the foam.
- **Brayden's bodyguard** follows him. If a shot misses near Brayden, he bundles Brayden toward the yacht early.

## The twist

**The foam party hides pops.** From 4:30, the pool deck fills with foam. A jelly popped in the foam is a puddle nobody can see, until the foam drains at the parasail phase and the eyes are found blinking in a puddle on the deck.

### The crowd

About 150 to 250 background jellies, by zone: beachgoers under umbrellas, spring breakers on the pool deck, tourists on the boardwalk, retirees on the pier. They copy what's near them (Brody's chants spread across the deck; a whistle turns every head). The final number comes from PERF-01.
